About the Festival

A space where art meets new expressions.

The National Festival of Digital Arts brings together visual arts, audiovisual creation and digital practices in a space dedicated to artistic exploration and contemporary expression.

This edition celebrates Algerian artistic creation and the artists exploring new possibilities through image, video, graphic design, light and immersive installations, while maintaining a strong connection with our cultural heritage.

Theme of the Festival

Algerian Imaginaries: creating in the digital age.

An exploration of how digital technologies are transforming the ways we create, see and experience art, without distancing us from our cultural heritage.

The Festival's Purpose

01

Supporting Algerian Creation

Giving visibility to Algerian artists and supporting contemporary artistic expression.

02

Building Artistic Bridges

Creating connections between visual arts and digital practices.

03

Encouraging Experimentation

Promoting artistic research, experimentation and creative collaborations.

04

Making Art Accessible

Making artworks accessible through cultural mediation and encounters with the public.
