The Programme

Four ways to explore, create and connect.

The festival unfolds through four complementary activities, bringing together artistic discovery, creative experimentation, exchange and knowledge sharing.

01

Exhibition

A journey through artistic expression.

Discover exclusive artworks and ongoing research exploring the possibilities of contemporary visual and digital creation.

The exhibition brings together different artistic disciplines, including video, animation, photography, illustration, painting and digital creations.

It also explores new forms of artistic expression through installations and projection mapping, depending on available technical resources.

Discover

  • Exclusive artworks.
  • Artistic research processes.
  • Digital and audiovisual creations.
  • Installations and projection mapping.
02

Creative Residency

Where ideas become new creations.

The creative residency invites artists to develop new artistic proposals through experimentation, research and the exploration of digital tools.

The resulting productions may take the form of artworks, audiovisual sequences or ongoing research processes presented to the public.

The residency is structured around two formats:

Supported Creative Residency

Four resident artists are welcomed and supported throughout the creative process, developing an artwork from beginning to end.

Augmented Artworks

Artists bring existing artworks that are enhanced through digital approaches and presented as part of the exhibition.

03

Talks & Meetings

Exchange ideas. Explore new perspectives.

A space for dialogue and reflection, bringing together different perspectives on the relationship between artistic creation, technology and cultural heritage.

Through an opening conference and panel discussions, the programme explores several key topics:

  • Digital Arts in Algeria.
  • Artistic Creation with Artificial Intelligence.
  • Algerian Heritage and Imaginaries.
  • The Production and Distribution of Digital Artworks.
04

Workshops

Learn. Experiment. Create.

Practical workshops designed to encourage experimentation, knowledge sharing and the discovery of new creative approaches.

The workshops explore different aspects of contemporary digital creation, including:

  • Developing an Artistic Concept.
  • Introduction to Projection Mapping.
  • Visual Creation with Artificial Intelligence.
  • Preparing an Artwork Presentation and Technical Sheet.
